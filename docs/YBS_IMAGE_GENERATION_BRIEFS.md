# YBS Image Generation Briefs

**Brand Visual Direction**: Photorealistic, cinematic yacht imagery with warm natural daylight, golden hour highlights, realistic luxury environments, and restrained professional atmosphere. Images must support the adjacent copy, never compete with it. Avoid artificial-looking yachts, inconsistent color grading, random stock photography, or embedded text overlays.

**Technical Standard**: All images should be high-resolution, professionally composed, and deliver a consistent luxury hospitality aesthetic. Aspect ratios and crops are tailored to each placement.

## Technical Notes

- All images should be exported as high-quality JPEGs (85–92% quality)
- Maintain warm color temperature (3500–4500K equivalent)
- Avoid artificial sharpening or over-processed HDR effects
- Ensure realistic yacht proportions and authentic luxury environments
- No text, logos, or graphic overlays embedded in images
- Images should load efficiently (optimize file size without visible quality loss)
- Provide 2x resolution for Retina/HiDPI displays where practical

---

**Final Reminder**: These images are content design, not decoration. Each one should reinforce the adjacent copy and brand positioning. When in doubt, choose restraint over spectacle, natural light over dramatic contrast, and authentic environments over contrived setups.
